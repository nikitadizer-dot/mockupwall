# Off-page / Backlink Playbook — Mockup Tool

Off-page authority is the single biggest lever we haven't pulled. Content is done;
rankings for competitive terms (e.g. `placeit alternative` pos ~40-54,
`clothing mockup generator` pos ~13) are now **authority-gated, not content-gated.**

**Reality check:** almost every quality directory is behind a Cloudflare/Turnstile
captcha, a Google/social-only login, or a paywall. Claude cannot bypass captchas
(owner's standing rule) or pay, so **most links require ~1-2 minutes of your manual
action each.** This playbook makes that as fast as possible: paste-ready copy + the
exact step per site. Budget ~30-40 min for Tier 1.
